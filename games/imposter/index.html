About Imposter Games

Imposter is a collection of social deduction games inspired by Among Us and other hidden role games. The core mechanic revolves around deception, trust, and logical deduction as players try to identify who among them is secretly working against the group.

Game Features

  • 🕵️ Multiple game modes with different mechanics
  • 👥 Support for various player counts
  • 🎯 Strategic voting and discussion phases
  • 🧠 Logic puzzles and deduction challenges
  • ⚡ Quick rounds for fast-paced gameplay
  • 🎨 Customizable themes and settings

How to Play

Each game mode follows the basic structure of social deduction: most players are crew members trying to complete objectives, while a few are imposters trying to sabotage them. Through discussion, voting, and gameplay, players must identify and eliminate the imposters before they succeed.
